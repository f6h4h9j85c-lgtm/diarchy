## Decisions
- 0.3.0, not 0.2.1: the CEO approved the additive protocol changes as a minor release (CEO, 2026-10-26). Also CEO: regenerate the samples for the default flow, build `diarchy doctor` and a one-command smoke test (local by default, optional `--github`), delete the leftover worktrees and probe folders, designer makes the icon.
- F033 derives the seen work evidence from the committed chat files and passes it to the pure engine, with no new budget.json field: a 0.2.0 plugin copy or global CLI reads an unknown field as corrupt, and mixed versions are likely while users upgrade. F126 likewise adds no field.
- F030 parks an idle PM instead of counting its clean exit. After PF1: a lead with a timed duty or a due DONE is never parked (it takes the 0.2.0 counted-restart path), a parked PM gets one uncounted relaunch at the park deadline (twice MAX_WAIT_MS), and a PM waiting on another session's lease does not count as running. A stall until WALL_CLOCK is the worst outcome, and the deadline costs at most one extra turn between messages.
- F154 steps over known launch wrappers on the gate side, with no owner-file format change.
- `diarchy start` and CLI `kickoff` refuse without diarchy.config.json: a missing config was the root cause of stray vaults, and the docs already required `diarchy init` first.
- Smoke defaults: transcripts included, redacted, with a printed notice; `--max-usd 3` per Claude process; `--minutes 20`.
- LC3 is a safety fix: tests must never spend real quota, so failing CLI stubs lead PATH and HOME and config directories are temp for every smoke test.
- README keeps "pre-alpha" until the first live two-model run. Tag v0.3.0 at release. Local branches stay; only worktrees are removed.
- F157's per-agent branch check and the coverage gaps judged design choices or unrealistic timing are closed with reasons in the addendum; their cheap guards shipped in V3-2.
- AC2 names 0.1.0 and 0.2.0 vault fixtures, so batch V3R-7 generated a real 0.2.0 fixture with the 30abe42 code.
- Every post-fix low survivor is fixed too (all cheap). The confirmation-audit lows and the two notes go in one batch, V3R-8-lows, with independent review and no further audit round: the confirmation audit already left no high or medium survivor, and each low fix has a test and a reviewer.
- The npm tarball keeps scripts/ (sync-skills, gen-samples, render-icon are small and inert); narrowing `files` gains users nothing and risks an install path.
